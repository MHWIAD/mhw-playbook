🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission: Create a safe, stigma-free space for IAD Adamstown associates to learn about, discuss, and support mental health & well-being.

Vision: Every associate on campus knows where to turn for mental health support and feels comfortable doing so.

Core Goals:

  1. Build awareness of mental health resources
  2. Reduce stigma through open conversation
  3. Train peer supporters (MHFA)
  4. Host regular wellness events
  5. Create a sustainable group structure
